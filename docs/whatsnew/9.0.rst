.. _whatsnew-9.0-scipy-special-quantity:

``scipy.special`` support for `~astropy.units.Quantity`
=======================================================

Most ufuncs from ``scipy.special`` can now be used directly with
:class:`~astropy.units.Quantity`. Functions are classified by their physical
semantics: angle-accepting functions (such as ``cosdg``, ``cosm1`` and
``ellipkinc``) correctly handle angle quantities, arithmetic-like functions
(such as ``agm``) preserve units, and the majority enforce dimensionless
inputs and outputs.
